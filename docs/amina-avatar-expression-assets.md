# Amina expression sample

The hackathon preview uses an eight-frame illustrative atlas at `public/mascots/amina-expression-atlas-v1.png`. It was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool using `public/mascots/amina.png` as the identity reference. The original artwork is unchanged.

The atlas is 1448 × 1086 pixels, with four columns and two rows. Reading left to right, its frames are idle, three speaking mouth shapes, listening, thoughtful, celebration, and greeting. CSS selects the frames and cycles approximate mouth shapes during scripted speech. It is not phoneme-aligned lip sync, rendered LemonSlice video, or proof of a live model connection. Reduced motion and the animation switch stop movement.

## Generation prompt

Create one transparent four-column, two-row sprite atlas of the referenced Amina character. Preserve her stylized 3D identity, curly high bun, gold glasses, cream shirt, orange waistcoat, lighting, proportions, and consistent waist-up framing. Top row: calm closed-mouth idle, small open-mouth speech, rounded vowel speech, and wider relaxed vowel speech. Bottom row: attentive listening, thoughtful hand-under-chin pose, cheerful celebration, and a friendly raised-hand greeting. Keep head position and scale consistent, all hands within each equal cell, and speech frames near-identical except their mouth shapes. No labels, borders, extra figures, or accessories.

## Tide appearance concept

The unlock explorer uses local sample counts, not earned learner Tides. Background and expression choices demonstrate proposed cosmetic rewards. Clothing/accessory options without artwork remain labelled concepts. They do not change agent intelligence, available learning techniques, or evidence requirements.
