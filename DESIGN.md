# Flowst Airs experience design

Status: working contract; see [verified scope](docs/references/verification.md).

Airs owns product navigation; Misu appears at planning/transitions, Amina during verbal practice, Kai during evidence review only when implemented. Product headers and navigation use Flowst Airs; Amina remains the verbal learning partner. Legacy links and stored agent IDs remain compatible.

Trust UX: clarity, predictability, consideration, control and honesty. Show actual processing, listening, speaking, saving and failure states. No invented progress, timing or thought streams. Explain coverage before planning; approve before practice. Surface concise explanations and inspectable evidence.

Use everyday activity names. Keep deeper rationale/provenance discoverable without interrupting conversation. Explain meaningful changes; learner can request help, stay or end. Accessibility and pace are choices, not classifications.

Report what is actually saved on failure, with working retries/fallbacks. State transcript errors/unseen visuals. Do not promise offline voice, multilingual fluency or measured gains without verification.
