# Product sense

Status: creator-aligned direction, not a measured efficacy claim.

Air helps independent learners examine what they retain, understand, explain and apply after consuming resources. AI-assisted code makes repository comprehension a concrete use case. The central goal is verbal development over time and confidence for career-defining conversations.

The [product brief](docs/product-specs/product-brief.md) is canonical. Source import is the entry journey; more formats alone do not establish the learning outcome.

Prioritize one manageable question, evidence from the attempt, requested support, teach-back and fresh application. Plans respect purpose, time and scope. Separate observation, tentative interpretation and practice recommendations.

Do not infer intelligence from accent, grammar conformity, speed or fluent repetition. Completion alone does not demonstrate retention, confidence or durable improvement. Development views need contextual cross-session evidence and delayed checks, with learner controls.

Misu/Amina foundation implemented; Kai/longitudinal continuity proposed; live provider checks separate from fixtures. Prefer a complete inspectable journey over unsupported breadth.
